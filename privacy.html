Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Welcome to Koushik HY's Portfolio accessible at https://koushikhy.netlify.app/. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing a high-performance, interactive cinematic experience. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by this website and how it is utilized in accordance with applicable data protection laws and advertising guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways depending on how you interact with our website:

  • Direct Contact Data: When you use our contact form or send us an email, you may voluntarily provide your name, email address, phone number, and message content. This information is used strictly to respond to your inquiries and is never sold or rented to third parties.
  • Log Files: Like standard web servers, our hosting provider (Netlify) uses log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: We may analyze anonymized usage data to evaluate web performance, 3D WebGL rendering compatibility, and navigation patterns.

2. Cookies, Web Beacons, and Advertising Partners

This website uses cookies to enhance user experience and serve relevant advertisements. A cookie is a small file stored on your device that tracks preference data.

4. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) have specific rights regarding their personal data:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data collected to another organization or directly to you.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Request that a business that sells personal data not sell the consumer's personal data. We do not sell personal information.

6. Children's Information Protection

Protecting children using the internet is an utmost priority. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and monitor their online activity. Koushik HY's Portfolio does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records.
